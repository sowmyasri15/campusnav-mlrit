# Presentation Script: MLRIT CampusNav System

**Total Duration:** ~3 Minutes

---

## 1. Introduction (30 Seconds)
"Good morning everyone. Today we are presenting **CampusNav**, a smart, real-time navigation and communication platform specifically built for the **MLR Institute of Technology** campus in Dundigal."

"Traditional maps are static. Our solution is dynamic. It doesn't just show you where to go; it understands the campus layout, calculates live walking paths, and keeps everyone safe and informed."

---

## 2. Core Navigation & The IT Block (60 Seconds)
"Let’s start with the heart of our campus. As you can see on the map, we have precisely mapped **20+ locations**, including a deep dive into the **Mahatma Gandhi Block**. This block isn't just a building; it houses our IT department, including two specialized **Centers of Excellence for IoT and Big Data**, and industry-aligned labs for **Full Stack Development and Cloud Computing**."

*(Action: Search for 'M&G Block' using Voice Search)*
"By simply using the **Voice Search** feature, I can say 'M&G Block', and the system instantly finds the IT department. I can then click 'Navigate Here' to get a precise walking route from my current location. The system uses the **OSRM engine** to draw the fastest path along our campus roads, showing the distance and estimated walking time."

---

## 3. Accessibility & Emergency Features (60 Seconds)
"Safety and inclusion are built-in. For students with different needs, the **Accessibility Toggle** filters the map to only show locations with elevator access and draws routes that avoid stairs. The route line even changes to green for easy identification."

*(Action: Click the Emergency Exit button)*
"In case of an emergency, every second counts. With one tap on the **Emergency Mode** button, the system automatically finds the nearest gate—whether it's the **Main Gate** or the **North Gate**—and draws a high-visibility red route to guide you out safely."

---

## 4. Real-time Announcements & Admin (30 Seconds)
"Finally, we have the **Live Announcements** feature. Campus administrators can use the secure **Admin Dashboard** to push urgent updates—like exam schedule changes or maintenance notices—which appear instantly on all student devices without a page refresh, powered by **Firebase Firestore**."

---

## 5. Conclusion (15 Seconds)
"CampusNav is more than just a map; it's a tool that makes MLRIT smarter and safer. It's ready for deployment, ready to use, and ready to scale. Thank you."
